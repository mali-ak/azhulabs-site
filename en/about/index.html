About

For years we have been writing software for companies and keeping their servers running. We started AzHu Labs to bring that experience to the phone in your pocket.

Working with complex systems every day has taught us what a good app looks like, and what it does not: one that gets to work the moment you open it, does not ask for permissions it does not need, and does not bury you in ads and notifications.

That is what we try to build. We start with apps that solve our own problems, then polish them until anyone can use them.

What we care about

  • Your data stays with you. No sign-up required; your records live on your device.
  • The app does its job. Every feature has to earn its place.
  • You get a real answer. Support e-mails are read and answered by the people who build the app.

What we have built so far

An IPTV player that works with your own playlist, an internet radio that also works in the car, a simple companion for daily dhikr, and an app that replaces the paper ledger of apartment managers. More are on the way.

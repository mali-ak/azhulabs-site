About

AzHu Labs is an independent software studio founded by two partners in Konya, Türkiye. We bring years of experience in enterprise software and systems administration to the apps we want to use ourselves.

We are a small team and we treat that as an advantage: each app does a few things well, asks for no unnecessary permissions, keeps your data on your device and opens fast.

The name comes from the first syllables of our children's names.

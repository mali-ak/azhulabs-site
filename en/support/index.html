Frequently asked questions

The app is not on the store, how do I install it?

Use the “Direct download” section on each app's page to get the APK or Windows installer. On Android you need to allow your browser to install from unknown sources.

How do I move my purchase to another device?

Sign in with the same Google or Apple account and tap “Restore purchases” in the app settings.

I want a refund.

Refunds for store payments follow Google Play / App Store policies; request it from the store first, and write to us if that fails.

I want to delete my data.

Our apps keep data on your device; uninstalling is enough. For apps with sync, turn sync off first.

Apartman Yönetim – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 October 2026 · AzHu Labs, Konya, Türkiye

Data we collect

Apartman Yönetim requires no account. It does not collect your name, e-mail address, location or contacts. Everything you enter in the app is stored only on your device.

Permissions

  • Internet: only for the services listed below.

Third-party services

  • Google Play (subscription): payments are processed by the store; we never see your payment details.
  • RevenueCat: validates subscription status and keeps an anonymous record under a random ID. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

Advertising

Apartman Yönetim shows no ads.

Children

Apartman Yönetim is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from children.

Deleting your data

Because data lives on your device, uninstalling the app removes all of it. The subscription record ends when you cancel the subscription in your store account.

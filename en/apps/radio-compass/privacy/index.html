Radio Compass – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 October 2026 · AzHu Labs, Konya, Türkiye

Data we collect

Radio Compass requires no account. It does not collect your name, e-mail address, location or contacts. Everything you enter in the app is stored only on your device.

Permissions

  • Internet: for the station list and audio streams.

Third-party services

  • Radio Browser (radio-browser.info): station list and logos come from this open directory; your searches are sent to it without any identifier.
  • Radio broadcasters: when you play a station your device connects directly to the broadcaster's server, which can see your IP address.

Advertising

Radio Compass shows no ads.

Children

Radio Compass is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from children.

Deleting your data

Because data lives on your device, uninstalling the app removes all of it.

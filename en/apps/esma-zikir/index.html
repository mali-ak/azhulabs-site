Esma Zikir

The 99 Names, a tasbih counter and reminders in one app.

Coming soon Android

Esma Zikir presents the 99 Names of Allah with meanings and numerological (abjad) values, and helps you keep a regular practice with a counter, intention groups and daily reminders.

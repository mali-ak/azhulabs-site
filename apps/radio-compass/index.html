Frequently asked questions

Where does the station list come from?

From the open, community-maintained Radio Browser directory. If a station does not play, the broadcaster has usually stopped the stream.

It does not show up in Android Auto.

Once installed from the store, Android Auto detects it automatically. Test builds require “Unknown sources” to be enabled in Android Auto developer settings.

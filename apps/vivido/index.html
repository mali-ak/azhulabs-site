Frequently asked questions

Does Vivido provide channels or movies?

No. Vivido is only a player; content comes from the playlists and servers you add. You are responsible for using sources you are entitled to access.

Which APK should I download?

Use the arm64 APK for phones and tablets, and the arm32 (armeabi-v7a) APK for Android TV / Google TV devices.

My Pro purchase is missing on a new device.

Sign in with the same store account and tap “Restore purchases” in settings. Google Play and App Store purchases cannot be transferred between each other.

How do I cancel the subscription?

In the Subscriptions section of your Google Play or App Store account. Pro stays active until the end of the paid period.
